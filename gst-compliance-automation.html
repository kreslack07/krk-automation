GST Compliance Automation for Sole Traders

If you cross A$75,000 turnover, you register for GST. Most AU sole traders hit that faster than they expect — and the BAS becomes a quarterly panic. Here's the 5-node workflow that fixes it.

The trigger for GST registration

ATO rule: register within 21 days of crossing A$75k turnover (excluding GST). Most sole traders don't track this in real time. The workflow below does.

The 5-node n8n workflow

  1. Trigger: Schedule (every Monday 9am AEDT)
  2. Aggregate: HTTP Request to Stripe — get all charges from the last 7 days, sum by currency, convert to AUD
  3. Compare: Function node — add to running YTD total (stored in Supabase), check if > A$75k
  4. Alert: If over threshold → send email to operator: "GST registration required. You crossed A$75k on [date]"
  5. Log: Write the weekly total to Supabase for the audit trail

Why this matters

Failure to register on time = ATO penalties + interest on unpaid GST. The penalty starts at 1 penalty unit per week (currently A$330) and compounds. The workflow pays for itself the first time it catches you approaching the threshold.

BAS-ready monthly report (bonus workflow)

Once registered, the same Stripe + Supabase pattern generates a monthly BAS draft:

  • Total sales (1A): from Stripe charges
  • GST collected (1B): total sales / 11
  • Total purchases (1C): from a parallel "Receipts" Sheet where you log expenses
  • GST paid (1D): purchases / 11
  • Net GST (1A - 1D): calculated

Manual input needed: log receipts in a Google Sheet as you incur them. The workflow does the rest. Estimated 4 hours/month saved vs. doing BAS by hand.

The catch

This isn't a substitute for an accountant. AU sole-trader tax is more than GST. But it removes 80% of the data-entry pain so the accountant gets clean numbers to work with.
