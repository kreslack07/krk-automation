n8n Workflows for Tradies

Five production-ready n8n workflows for AU tradies (electricians, plumbers, builders, HVAC). Each one saves 2-5 hours/week. Total combined build: 8-12 hours if you do it yourself, A$2,000-A$3,000 if you hire it.

Workflow 1: Lead capture from website form

Stack: Website form → n8n webhook → Gmail + Google Sheets + SMS notification

Saves: 30 min/day. The form fills, the email is sent to the tradie's inbox with the customer's details, the row is added to a "Leads" Sheet, and an SMS arrives on the tradie's phone. No CRM subscription needed.

Build time: 2 hours. Cost: A$0 (all free tiers).

Workflow 2: Gmail triage

Stack: Gmail Trigger → Filter by category → Apply labels

Saves: 1 hour/day. The workflow auto-labels incoming email as job-quote, supplier, customer, or personal based on keywords + sender domain. Tradie's inbox becomes 4 quick-scan folders instead of 200 unread messages.

Build time: 1 hour. Cost: A$0.

Workflow 3: ServiceM8 ↔ Google Calendar two-way sync

Stack: ServiceM8 webhook → Google Calendar event; Google Calendar update → ServiceM8 job update

Saves: 45 min/day. Tradies using ServiceM8 for job management can see their day in Google Calendar (which most people already use for personal events). Two-way sync means a calendar change on phone updates the ServiceM8 job.

Build time: 3 hours. Cost: A$0.

Workflow 4: Auto review request after job complete

Stack: ServiceM8 webhook ("job complete") → 3-day delay → SMS via Twilio → Google Sheet for tracking

Saves: 2 hours/week. The single highest-ROI workflow for tradies. Most happy customers leave a review IF asked, but tradies forget to ask. The workflow asks for them, 3 days after job completion, with a friendly SMS and a one-tap link to Google Reviews.

Build time: 2 hours. Cost: A$0.05/SMS (Twilio). For 20 jobs/mo, that's A$1/mo.

Workflow 5: Invoice follow-up dunning

Stack: Schedule (daily) → Stripe pending invoices → Filter by age → Email template per stage

Saves: 3 hours/week + recovers A$500-A$2,000/mo in unpaid invoices. Most tradies chase unpaid invoices manually, inconsistently. The workflow sends a friendly nudge at 7 days, a firmer one at 14, and a final notice at 21. It runs every day without you thinking about it.

Build time: 2 hours. Cost: A$0.

Combined: 8-12 hours of build time, ~10 hours/week saved

The math: 10 hours/week × A$50/hr tradie time = A$500/week value. Payback in 1-2 weeks. After that, it's pure time-saved.
