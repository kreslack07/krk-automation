AI Lead Enrichment from a Google Sheet

The most-requested workflow I build. Input: a Sheet of 500 company names. Output: the same Sheet with verified email, decision-maker name, and LinkedIn URL appended. Time: 7 days for the build, then it runs in the background.

Why this matters

B2B lead enrichment is a $4B/year industry. The reason: most companies have a list of "leads" that are really just company names. To actually contact a human, you need (a) their work email, (b) the decision-maker's name, and (c) a way to verify the email isn't bouncing.

Doing this manually is 5-10 min per lead. For 500 leads, that's 40-80 hours. The workflow does it in 4-6 hours of build time + 1-2 hours of automated run time.

The 6-node n8n workflow

  1. Google Sheets Trigger — new row added to "Leads" tab
  2. Apollo API call — company name → people search for relevant decision-makers (title filter: CEO, Founder, Owner, GM)
  3. Apollo API call — for each person, get the email (Apollo's "reveal" endpoint)
  4. OpenRouter — LLM cleans the name (handles "John Smith" vs "Smith, John" vs "J Smith" disambiguation) and writes a 1-sentence context note
  5. Google Sheets — write back: email, full name, title, LinkedIn URL, context note
  6. Optional: Slack/email notification to sales rep with the enriched lead

Cost per lead

  • Apollo credits: 1 per lead (free tier gives 10,000 credits/mo)
  • OpenRouter tokens: ~A$0.002 per lead (gpt-4o-mini for the cleansing step)
  • n8n executions: 1 per lead (free tier covers 200/mo)

Total: roughly A$0.50/lead (mostly Apollo credits). For 500 leads: A$250. Vs A$2,000-$4,000 for a ZoomInfo subscription. Same data, 1/10 the cost.

Privacy & compliance (CRITICAL)

AU Privacy Act 1988 + GDPR apply. B2B contact data is "personal information" if it identifies a natural person. To stay on the right side of the law:

  • Only enrich leads you have a legitimate business relationship with (inbound form fill, referral, public listing)
  • Don't enrich for the purpose of unsolicited cold outreach (that's spam under Spam Act 2003)
  • Document the source of each lead in a "LeadSource" column in the Sheet
  • Provide a clear opt-out: if someone asks to be removed, remove them within 30 days

Most B2B enrichment services (Apollo, ZoomInfo, Lusha) operate in a grey area here. The "have a legitimate business relationship" rule is the safe path.

The catch

Apollo's free tier gives 10,000 credits/mo, but the "email reveal" feature is gated behind a paid tier (~$49/mo). For 500+ leads/mo, you'll need the paid Apollo tier. For under 500, the free tier + 50% reveal rate is workable.
