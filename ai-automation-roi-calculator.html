AI Automation ROI Calculator

For each task you're considering automating, work through the 4 numbers below. The answer is usually obvious by the time you finish.

The 4 numbers

  1. Hours saved per week — the realistic estimate, not the optimistic one. If you think you save 5 hours/week, your 2-week average is probably 2 hours/week.
  2. Hourly value to your business — for AU sole traders, A$50-100/hr is reasonable. For tradies with billable time, A$80-150/hr. For consultants, A$100-300/hr.
  3. Build cost — A$500-A$2,000 if hired, or 4-12 hours of your time if you DIY.
  4. Ongoing maintenance hours per month — usually 0.5-2 hours/mo for a stable workflow. Ignore this for the first 6 months; you won't have meaningful data.

The worksheet

Fill in the numbers above and click Calculate.

Heuristics

  • Payback < 30 days: build it. The math is obvious.
  • Payback 30-90 days: build it if you can afford the upfront cost, or DIY if you have the time.
  • Payback 90-180 days: build it only if you'll actually use it consistently. Most "I'll do it later" workflows get abandoned.
  • Payback > 180 days: probably not worth it. The opportunity cost of your attention is usually higher than the savings.

The trap to avoid

Don't count "hours I'd save if I had to do this task from scratch." Count hours you actually do it, today, on average. The gap between those two numbers is where most automation ROI claims go to die.
