Podcasting: AI Transcription + Show Notes Pricing AU

What does it actually cost to transcribe a 45-minute podcast episode in Australia in 2026? Here's a real comparison, and the n8n workflow that does the AI version for A$0.30 in compute.

The n8n + Whisper workflow

  1. Trigger: New MP3 uploaded to a specific Google Drive folder (or a Zapier-style "send to webhook" link)
  2. Whisper: POST the audio to OpenRouter's whisper-1 endpoint, get back the transcript with timestamps
  3. OpenRouter (LLM): Generate show notes (200-300 words) + chapter markers (3-5) + 3 tweet-length pull-quotes
  4. Write back: Create a Google Doc with the transcript + show notes, return the URL via email

Total time: 3-5 min for a 45-min episode. Total cost: ~A$0.30 in OpenRouter tokens.

When to use which

  • Human (Rev): when the episode has heavy accents, multiple languages, or technical jargon that AI mangles
  • AI (self-built): for the 90% case — clean audio, English-only, single or dual speakers
  • AI + light human review: the sweet spot. AI does the heavy lift, you spend 10 min cleaning up names and any terms the AI hallucinated

The show notes multiplier

The real value of transcription isn't the text — it's the SEO. A 45-min episode becomes:

  • 3,000-5,000 words of transcript (Google ranks transcript text)
  • A 300-word blog post (the show notes)
  • 3-5 tweet-length pull-quotes (social media)
  • 5-7 LinkedIn posts (one per key insight)
  • A YouTube description (ranked by YouTube search)

That's 10-15 derivative content pieces from one episode. The AI workflow does this for A$0.30. The human workflow does it for A$90-$180 — and the marginal quality difference doesn't justify 300-600x the cost for most podcasts.

The catch

AI transcription has a 3-5% word error rate on clean audio, 8-12% on noisy audio. For a podcast you're publishing as-is, that's acceptable. For a legal deposition, it isn't. Know your use case.
